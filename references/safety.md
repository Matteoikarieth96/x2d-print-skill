# Safety for first-time users

Say the points that apply, in plain words, before the person sends the print.

## Before printing

- **Stable place:** the printer stands on a firm, level surface, with space around it and
  nothing on top of it. Never block the vents.
- **Children and pets** stay away while it runs.
- **Materials:** start with **PLA**. It prints at low temperature and gives off far fewer
  fumes than ABS or ASA. ABS, ASA, PC and nylon need a ventilated room and the X2D's
  filter; they are not for a first print. The X2D's filter reduces odours but does not
  make a printer a good roommate: avoid printing in a bedroom.
- **Plate:** clean and correctly seated against its stops. Do not touch the print
  surface with fingers (grease stops the first layer sticking). Wash it with warm water
  and dish soap when prints stop sticking; no acetone.

## While printing

- **Hot parts:** the nozzle reaches up to 300 °C, the bed up to 120 °C, and the chamber
  can be heated. Do not touch the toolhead, the bed or anything moving.
- **Stay for the first layer** (5 to 10 minutes). Most failures show there: not sticking,
  nozzle dragging, wrong colour.
- **Do not leave a print unattended** for long, especially the first prints, and never
  overnight when nobody is in the building.
- **Stop if anything is wrong:** pause or stop from the printer screen or from Bambu
  Studio's Device tab. A "spaghetti" mess, a burning smell or a grinding sound: stop.
- **Door:** for PLA, Bambu Studio and the printer manage chamber and fans; if Bambu
  Studio suggests opening the door or lid for PLA (high bed temperature), do it.

## After printing

- **Wait** until the plate cools to room temperature. Lift the plate out, bend it gently,
  the part pops off. Use a scraper only flat and away from fingers.
- Remove the prime tower and the purge line from the plate, then put the plate back
  against its stops.
- **Maintenance** only with the printer switched off, unplugged and cold.

## Printed parts

- Printed parts are not safe for loads that could hurt someone if they fail, for food,
  or for children's mouths. Test functional parts before trusting them.
